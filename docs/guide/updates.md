# Updates

[← Documentation](../README.md)

The launcher checks for new versions of the app when it starts and every six hours. When one is
out, it shows a banner and the release notes; **View release** opens the download page.
Download the new DMG and replace **Bloodborne** in Applications: your saves, profiles and
settings stay in `~/Library/Application Support/bloodborne_mac`. The launcher never updates
itself or touches your saves.

**Advanced → Updates** shows your version, has **Check now**, and can turn the automatic checks
off. Checks send nothing about you or your saves, and never block Play when you are offline.
