# Bloodborne for Apple Silicon 0.8.1

What's new:

- **GX worker crash fixed:** changing dynamic shadows while playing could crash the game during
  loading or a camera turn, on Metal or MoltenVK. The repeatable crash is fixed.
- **Shader cache reused:** a rejected tessellation pipeline no longer makes the shader cache
  rebuild at every launch, avoiding the related brief FPS dips. The corrected shaders rebuild
  once after updating.
- **MetalFX (Apple):** a new optional upscaler with Quality, Balanced and other presets, on
  either renderer. The big FPS counter shows MetalFX and its render and output resolutions.

## GX worker crash

The live dynamic-shadows setting could turn shadows back on in a secondary render context that
has no shadow target. Its worker could then crash. The setting still changes live in normal
scenes; the game now keeps shadows off where that context requires it. The copied-save crash was
reproduced on Metal and MoltenVK before the fix. Both renderers passed copied-save tests
afterwards, including a five-minute Metal run with left turns. Metal also keeps shader and
pipeline objects alive until its queued draws finish.

## Shader cache

A tessellation shader could assign the same output location twice, causing MoltenVK to reject
its pipeline. That rejection was also reported as a “damaged” shader cache. The shader is
corrected, and rejected pipelines are no longer saved for the next launch. The shader cache
rebuilds once after this update to replace older generated shaders; the next launch should
reuse it. This stops the brief FPS dips caused by repeatedly compiling the affected pipelines.

## MetalFX (Apple)

Choose **Graphics → Upscaling → MetalFX (Apple)** in the launcher or in-game menu. It works with
either renderer on supported Macs and uses the same Quality, Balanced and other presets. FSR 3.1
remains the default. In one copied-save scene on an M3 Pro at 1080p Balanced, FSR's median frame
time was 21.3 ms and MetalFX's was 23.9 ms; both had about 28–29 ms p90. Choose the picture you
prefer. MetalFX is experimental; please report visual errors or stalls with the game log.

## Known issues

- Characters can be drawn black above about 3.5 million render pixels. Use a lower render
  resolution or upscaler preset if this happens.
- Rare sampler/GPU assertions and movie shutdown faults seen in earlier development runs remain
  on the watch list.

## Good to know

- The Metal renderer and MetalFX are experimental.
- The game's on-screen button icons are always PlayStation's, also with the keyboard or an Xbox
  pad.

## Install or update

Download the Apple Silicon DMG and replace Bloodborne.app. Saves and settings remain in
`~/Library/Application Support/bloodborne_mac`. No game files are included: supply your own
Bloodborne dump with update 1.09. The app includes its runtime, bash, Python and libraries.
