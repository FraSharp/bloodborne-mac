# Bloodborne for Apple Silicon 0.8.0

What's new:

- **Crash with motion vectors fixed:** character motion vectors no longer end the game with a GPU
  "Invalid Resource" error. They make the image much sharper on moving characters for a small
  cost, so they are worth turning on again.
- **One set of settings:** the launcher and the in-game menu now share every setting, and a change
  in one shows up in the other while you play.
- **A new in-game menu** in the launcher's look, with its tabs and every graphics setting, and an
  on-screen keyboard for the controller.
- **Outputs above 1080p start again** (1440p and 4K stopped at the loading screen on 18 GB Macs).
- **Metal renderer (experimental):** a new option draws the game with Apple's Metal directly
  instead of through MoltenVK, usually faster.
- **FPS counter** in two sizes and six positions, and a **memory estimate** for your settings.
- **Trophy notifications** show only over the game and no longer take its focus.
- **From bbport 0.6:** a frame rate that keeps physics right at any speed, with a live limit and
  **F3** for 30 FPS; effects that switch without a restart (and a new **Bloom** switch);
  Laurence's cinematic fixed above 30 FPS; fewer single black or grey frames.

## Motion vectors

With character motion vectors on, the game could stop with a GPU "Invalid Resource" error
(reported as `VK_ERROR_OUT_OF_DEVICE_MEMORY`), for example in a boss fight. Metal's own checks
named the cause: GPU objects were freed while the GPU still needed them, in three places (memory
taken out of the resident set, texture deletions ahead of the draws that still used them, and
large staging buffers MoltenVK still referred to). Each is now released only after the work that
uses it has finished. The boss fight from a backup that crashed was won with them on, and a
20-minute session ran without a crash.

## Settings and the in-game menu

- The launcher keeps the settings in its database, shared by all profiles. While the game runs,
  a change in the launcher reaches the game, and a change in the in-game menu reaches the launcher.
- The menu (**F1**, or **L3 + R3** on a controller) has the launcher's look and its tabs:
  **Graphics**, **Game** and **Advanced**, with every graphics setting the launcher has, including
  fullscreen, monitor, frame presentation, frame-rate mode and limit, and the performance
  options. Fullscreen switches at once; settings that need a restart say so, and **Restart**
  applies them.
- **L1/R1** switch tabs and **Circle** closes the menu. Choices too long for their row become
  steppers.
- **On-screen keyboard:** typing the character name with a controller (D-pad or stick to move,
  Cross types, Square deletes, Triangle adds a space, R1 shift, L1 symbols).
- **FPS counter:** small (frame rate and frame time) or big (also what draws and runs the game,
  the upscaler, the resolutions and a frame-time graph), in any corner or edge center (Graphics →
  Frame rate). Its number no longer moves as it changes.
- **Memory estimate** on the Graphics page and in the menu: what the game will use with your
  settings, against your Mac's memory, in amber above three quarters of it.
- **Metal performance HUD** (Graphics → Frame rate): Apple's statistics overlay.

## Above 1080p

1440p and 4K outputs stopped at the loading screen on an 18 GB Mac: the GPU device was lost. The
game was given 9 GB of memory for any output above 1080p, and the GPU saw it twice, about 17 GB
in all. Now the GPU sees the memory once, in overlapping pieces, and the game gets only what its
picture needs (5–6 GB with an upscaler preset). With FSR, every output stays under 8 GB.

## Metal renderer (experimental)

**Advanced → Performance → Renderer → Metal (experimental)** draws the game through Apple's Metal
directly instead of translating Vulkan through MoltenVK. In two alternating pairs in the Hunter's
Dream (Apple M3 Pro, 1080p, FSR Balanced) it ran at 65.5 and 61.7 FPS, against 58.0 and 58.1 on
MoltenVK, with steadier frame times, and a full play session on it was stable. MoltenVK stays the
default; switch back if anything looks wrong, and please report it. The Metal renderer is adapted
from [Wooloomooloo2/bloodborne_mac_port](https://github.com/Wooloomooloo2/bloodborne_mac_port)
(itself partly from Huax-1839/bloodborne-macos); see the
[native renderer notes](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-runtime/blob/main/docs/NATIVE_RENDERER.md).

## From bbport 0.6

These come from [bbport](https://github.com/deadinside28/bloodborne_pc) 0.6 by deadinside28,
ported to this Mac version.

- **Frame rate.** Above 30 FPS the game now steps by each frame's real time instead of using the
  community frame-rate patches: physics timers, cloth, damping and running speed stay right at any
  frame rate. The frame-rate limit applies while you play, and **F3** switches a 30 FPS limit on
  and off. In our tests it ran as fast as the patches.
- **Effects without a restart:** chromatic aberration, depth of field, motion blur, ambient
  occlusion, the game's anti-aliasing, shadows from dynamic lights and the new **Bloom** switch
  change at once, in the launcher or the in-game menu. Screen-space reflections still need a
  restart. Ambient occlusion is no longer forced where the game turned it off (a crash after
  selecting an item).
- **Laurence's cinematic** no longer hangs or loses models above 30 FPS.
- **Fewer single black or grey frames:** the picture waits for its frame to be finished before
  it is shown.
- Rare crashes now logged and skipped instead: a blur-kernel check, a full per-frame GPU command
  heap, stale texture formats, damaged GPU commands, missing vertex buffers.

## Trophy notifications

Notifications now show only while the game's window is in front, so they no longer appear over
other apps, and they no longer wait for the game to take focus. If the notification helper ever
becomes the active app, it hands focus straight back to the game.

## Known issues

- **Characters drawn black at very high render sizes:** when the game renders more than about 3.5
  million pixels (1440p without an upscaler or with Native AA, 4K with Quality), characters are
  drawn black. Use an upscaler preset that renders less (1440p Balanced, 4K Balanced). The cause
  is still being investigated.
- The Metal renderer is experimental.
- Offline play on macOS. Online integration is not included.
- Rare sampler/GPU assertions and movie shutdown faults seen in earlier development runs remain
  on the watch list.
- The save editor is still in development.

## Install or update

Download the Apple Silicon DMG and replace Bloodborne.app. Saves and settings remain in
`~/Library/Application Support/bloodborne_mac`; the launcher imports your existing settings
on its first start. No game files are included: supply your own Bloodborne dump with update 1.09.
The app includes its runtime, bash, Python and libraries.
